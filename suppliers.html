WHAT MMGC LOOKS FOR

Compatibility matters more than price alone.

Technical complianceExact model, specification, standard and required accessories.
Commercial valueCompetitive pricing, quotation validity and clear tax treatment.
Delivery capabilityReliable lead time, freight arrangements and Lesotho delivery where needed.
After-sales supportWarranty, product support and manufacturer/reseller documentation where relevant.

HOW THE MARKETPLACE WORKS

01

02

MMGC reviews

We assess fit for current and future sourcing needs.

03

Quote requests

Suitable suppliers may receive item-specific quotation requests.

04

Technical check

Products are checked against tender or customer specifications.

05

Commercial comparison

Price, warranty, lead time, delivery and documentation are compared.

06

Procurement decision

MMGC selects the most compatible supplier for the requirement, not automatically the lowest price.
