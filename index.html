ABOUT MMGC

Built around accuracy, communication and accountability.

MMGC General Trading is a Lesotho-owned multidisciplinary enterprise established to respond to the documentation, sourcing and operational needs of businesses, public-sector suppliers and project teams.

We combine hands-on administrative support with general trading capability, allowing clients to work with one responsive provider for tender preparation, quotations, supplier sourcing, document production, IT support, general supplies, cleaning and selected technical field services.

Vision

To become a trusted Lesotho enterprise recognised for reliable business support, responsible trading and practical solutions.

Mission

To deliver responsive, accurate and value-conscious services through professional administration, dependable sourcing and accountable execution.
