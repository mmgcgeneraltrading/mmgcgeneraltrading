IMPORTANT BOQ CAUTION

Final arithmetic remains the bidder's responsibility.

For selected tenders MMGC may assist with BOQ review, pricing and calculations. The client/bidder remains responsible for independently proofing and confirming final BOQ totals, quantities, arithmetic and the tendered amount before submission.
