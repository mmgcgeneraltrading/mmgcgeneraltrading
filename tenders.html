MMGC COSTING DESK

From tender specification to realistic market cost.

For tenders with freely available bidding documents, MMGC can extract items, quantities and specifications and prepare an estimated cost using high-quality evidence such as manufacturers, authorised distributors, established suppliers and reputable retailers. Where appropriate, logistics, warranty, VAT and delivery costs can also be considered.

Client responsibility: MMGC costing is support information. For selected tenders, the client/bidder must proof and confirm the final BOQ total and tendered amount before submission.
Public document availableMMGC reviews it and calculates an estimated market cost from the actual specification.
Tender document must be purchasedThe advert is marked as paid. Costing begins after the complete purchased bidding document is provided.
Advert only / incomplete specificationNo invented price. MMGC identifies what is missing before producing a defensible estimate.
Highest-quality sourcingPreference goes to manufacturer, authorised reseller/distributor and established market sources.
