MMGC COSTING DESK

Know the likely cost before you price the bid.

Where bidding documents are publicly available, MMGC can work from the actual specifications and seek high-quality market evidence from manufacturers, authorised distributors and established suppliers. For paid bidding documents, costing begins once the complete document is available.

IMPORTANT BOQ CAUTION

Final arithmetic remains the bidder's responsibility.

For selected tenders MMGC may assist with BOQ review, pricing and calculations. The client/bidder remains responsible for independently proofing and confirming final BOQ totals, quantities, arithmetic and the tendered amount before submission.
