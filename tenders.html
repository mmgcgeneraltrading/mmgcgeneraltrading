MMGC COSTING DESK

From tender specification to realistic market cost.

For tenders with freely available bidding documents, MMGC can extract the items, quantities and specifications and prepare an estimated cost using high-quality evidence such as manufacturers, authorised distributors, established suppliers and reputable retailers. Where suitable, logistics, warranty, VAT and delivery costs can also be considered.

Public document availableMMGC can review it and prepare a cost estimate from the specification.
Tender document must be purchasedThe advert will state that the document is paid. Costing begins after the complete purchased document is provided.
Advert only / incomplete specificationNo invented price. MMGC will state what information is missing before giving a defensible estimate.
Supplier evidencePreference is given to manufacturer, authorised reseller/distributor and established market sources rather than low-quality listings.
